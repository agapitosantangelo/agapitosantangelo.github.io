Projects

Active research projects at the intersection of causal policy evaluation and digital trace data. Each project combines a substantive policy question with a methodological contribution — usually a new use of web, social-media or administrative data integrated with quasi-experimental design.

EU Cohesion Policy & Digital Public Services

Working paper · In submission

A causal evaluation of how EU Cohesion funds shape the supply of digital public services across Italian municipalities. Combines large-scale web scraping of municipal websites with administrative funding data; identifies effects through a generalized Difference-in-Differences design exploiting the staggered rollout of cohesion funding.

With
N. Caravaggio, G. Resce
Outputs
ESDP No. 25100 working paper · CARMA 2024 conference paper · journal submission in progress
Data
~8,000 Italian municipalities · ESF/ERDF programmes 2014–2020
DiD · Web scraping · EU policy · Public services digitalization

Nowcasting Food Insecurity with Multi-platform Digital Data

Published · Ongoing

A multi-platform framework that combines text mining of public discourse, social media data, and structured indicators to nowcast food insecurity at country level. Demonstrates that digital traces can detect food security shocks earlier than traditional survey-based indicators.

With
B. Carneiro, G. Resce, G. Tucci, G. Ruscica, N. Caravaggio, L. Fanelli, P. Cruciata, K. Coffey
Funder
International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIAT)
Outputs
Scientific Reports (2025) · two CARMA 2024 conference papers
Text mining · Machine learning · Nowcasting · Food security · Development

Fiscal Constraints & Health Inequality Research

Working paper

A bibliometric perspective on how fiscal constraints shape the production of academic research on health inequality. Maps the research landscape and identifies gaps where weakened fiscal capacity may be limiting the evidence base for health-equity policy.

With
F. Pietrovito, A. Rancan, G. Resce
Outputs
ESDP No. 26102 working paper
Bibliometrics · Health inequality · Public economics

Digital Communication of Italian Municipalities (PNRR)

Ongoing

Web-based study of how Italian municipalities communicate about the Italian National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR). Uses readability measures and text mining on official municipal communication to detect heterogeneity in transparency and engagement, with a particular focus on territorial disparities.

Outputs
Eticaeconomia article · paper in preparation
Data
Web-scraped corpus of Italian municipal websites and social-media accounts
PNRR · Text readability · Local government · Public communication

Detecting Fake Innovators in the Italian Startup Ecosystem

Conference paper · In progress

A web-scraping pipeline combined with supervised classification to identify firms registered as innovative startups under Italian law that show no real innovation activity online. Best models reach AUC ~0.91 on a labelled validation set, offering a low-cost screening tool for policy-makers.

With
I. Nigro, M. Modina
Outputs
CARMA 2025 conference paper · journal version in preparation
Machine learning · Web scraping · Innovation policy · Italy

Public Interest in Environmental Issues — Google Trends

Conference paper · In progress

Uses Google Trends search data to study the dynamics of public attention to environmental issues across countries and time. Combines search-based attention measures with structural country-level controls to assess whether public interest is in fact declining or being displaced by other concerns.

With
E. Cebrián Cerdá, J. Domenech (Universitat Politècnica de València)
Outputs
CARMA 2025 conference paper · journal version in preparation
Google Trends · Environmental concern · Cross-country panel
